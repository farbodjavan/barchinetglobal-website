About / Barchinet Inc.

Human needs.
Ideas we can build on.

Barchinet connects architectural work, research and tool development around a shared purpose: turning people’s needs and choices into designs and buildable changes for the places they inhabit.

Built across disciplines.

The founders shape ideas, design the product and participate in its development together. Their work brings architectural and business experience into contact with programming, research coordination and technical exploration.

Today’s workspace, developing tools and long-term habitat vision are connected parts of that direction, with different levels of readiness.

“A habitat design ecosystem, from Earth to space”

Our direction—from practical architectural work toward broader possibilities for participation.
