Website privacy

This policy describes barchinetglobal.com, operated by Barchinet Inc. The separate app privacy policies describe our offline Android calculators.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this website. Analytics records pages viewed, referral sources, basic browser and device information, approximate geographic location, and visits over time using cookie identifiers. It does not tell us your name. Google processes the website analytics data on its infrastructure.

The Google analytics tag is blocked until you select Accept analytics. Reject analytics keeps it blocked. You can change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Your preference is stored on your browser for 180 days; clearing browser storage removes it. If you withdraw permission after accepting, our tracking is disabled and we remove the analytics cookies we can access. Previously received data is not automatically deleted by changing your browser preference.

What we do not send

We do not configure advertising personalization, Google Signals, user IDs, or collection of form contents. Our tracking does not send email addresses, project entries or URL fragments, and strips query parameters except simple campaign tags. Contact clicks are measured only as an email-contact action, without the recipient address or message. Outbound links are measured by destination domain.

Hosting and contact

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process connection information when delivering pages, independently of optional analytics. If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send so that we can respond.

Your choices and further information

You may use the website without accepting analytics. Learn how Google processes information from websites that use its services or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. For a privacy request, contact FarbodFazaeliJavan@barchinetglobal.com. Do not send sensitive project or identity documents in a privacy inquiry.
