Website privacy

This policy describes barchinetglobal.com and its Barchinet Campus page at campus.barchinetglobal.com, operated by Barchinet Inc.: the public website, accounts and sign-in, and the signed-in workspace. The separate app privacy policies describe our offline Android calculators.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use the public pages of this website. Analytics records pages viewed, referral sources, basic browser and device information, approximate geographic location, and visits over time using cookie identifiers. It does not tell us your name. Google processes the website analytics data on its infrastructure. Analytics is not used on the sign-in pages or in the workspace.

The Google analytics tag is blocked until you select Accept analytics. Reject analytics keeps it blocked. You can change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Your preference is stored on your browser for 180 days; clearing browser storage removes it. If you withdraw permission after accepting, our tracking is disabled and we remove the analytics cookies we can access. Previously received data is not automatically deleted by changing your browser preference.

What we do not send

We do not configure advertising personalization, Google Signals, user IDs, or collection of form contents. Our tracking does not send email addresses, project entries or URL fragments, and strips query parameters except simple campaign tags. Contact clicks are measured only as an email-contact action, without the recipient address or message. Outbound links are measured by destination domain.

Accounts and sign-in

You can create an account with your email address and a password, or continue with your Google account. Sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication, a Google service: it holds your email address, your password (for email and password accounts) or your Google account’s identity (for Google sign-in), and issues the sign-in tokens our website checks. Firebase also sends the email for choosing a new password and, where used, the email address confirmation link. The Firebase sign-in library stores your sign-in state in your browser while you use Google sign-in.

When you create an account with a password, we email you a six-digit code to confirm your address. That email is sent through Resend, a transactional email service, which receives your email address and the message. We keep only a protected form of the code, and it expires after 10 minutes.

Our sign-in cookies are strictly necessary: one keeps you signed in for up to an hour at a time, one keeps an email and password sign-in for up to 60 days so you are not asked again, one lasts 20 minutes while you confirm your address, and one remembers that you signed out. They are not used for analytics or advertising.

What we keep about your account

Accounts and the workspace run on Cloudflare: the website and its sign-in service are hosted using Cloudflare Workers, and account and workspace records are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. We keep:

  • Your account: its identifier, your email address, and whether and when your account was approved, accepted as a guest, declined or suspended.
  • The country you said you live in now, which you choose when you create an account (and can change in My profile), and the time you chose it.
  • The record that you ticked “I confirm I am 18 or older.” when you created your account, with the time. You must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • If you are a member: the profile you choose to fill in (display name, role, organization, interests, bio, website, education), your requests to join projects (with the contributor terms version you accepted and when), and what you add in the workspace: tasks, comments, articles, submissions, Design Studio designs and the files you upload.
  • Short-lived security records: counts of sign-in, sign-up, code and posting attempts, keyed by your IP address, your email address or your account, deleted after two days; and a project you chose with “Join this project” before your account was approved, deleted when it is used or after 30 days.

Cloudflare also reports the country your connection comes from. We use it only to decide whether the public website opens without an account (it does in the United States and Canada); we do not store it.

Approval, guest accounts and owner actions

Barchinet reviews new accounts. Accounts with a confirmed university email address from people who live in the United States or Canada are approved automatically. For people who live elsewhere, Barchinet usually accepts the account as a guest account. A guest account is signed in and can browse the public website; it has no workspace, Design Studio, dashboard or project joining, and no member features. Barchinet can later approve a guest account as a membership, or make a member a guest again. The same account records described above are kept for guest accounts; a guest account cannot add workspace content.

Barchinet keeps a record of owner actions: which Barchinet administrator approved, declined, accepted as a guest, suspended or restored an account, or changed project, team access, release or Community settings, the account or project concerned, and when. It is used to check and correct decisions.

Who sees what

Other members see your display name next to your work, or a neutral label if you have not set one. They do not see your email address. Barchinet’s administrators see members’ email addresses so they can review accounts and manage project access. Visitors to the public pages and the Barchinet Campus page do not see member names or profiles. For contributions Barchinet accepts, the contributor terms describe how you are credited.

Hosting and contact

This website is hosted using Cloudflare Workers. Hosting providers may process connection information when delivering pages, independently of optional analytics. If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send so that we can respond. Before changing the structure of the account database, Barchinet exports a backup copy of it and keeps that copy privately.

Your choices, deletion and further information

You may use the public website without accepting analytics. Learn how Google processes information from websites that use its services or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

You can change your profile and the country you live in at any time in My profile. To ask for a copy of your account information, a correction, or the deletion of your account and its records, contact FarbodFazaeliJavan@barchinetglobal.com from the email address of your account. Contributions Barchinet has already accepted stay with their project as the contributor terms describe. Do not send sensitive project or identity documents in a privacy inquiry.
