          How joining works

          Three steps. The project you pick stays with you the whole way, through sign-in, sign-up and approval.

          1. 1

            Pick a building

            Choose a project on the campus or in the list, and press Join this project.

            The project you pick

          2. 2

            Sign in or make an account

            You must be 18 or older. A confirmed university email is approved automatically; other accounts are approved by Barchinet.

            Still the project you pick

          3. 3

            Land in that project

            You arrive at the project you picked, with its join request open. Send it, and you start on the tasks once you are accepted.

            At the project you picked

          After you ask to join

          Barchinet reads your request and accepts or declines it. Once you are in, each project has its own reviewers for the work you submit, and Barchinet makes the final decision on what is accepted.

          Joining is always free.

          What your students do and keep

          • Real tasks on a research question.

            Students work in the signed-in workspace: project tasks, reviews, resources, articles, publishing and a browser 3D Design Studio.

          • Work they can show.

            They own the part they contribute. Accepted work names them on the project, unless they ask not to be named, and they may cite it in a portfolio, CV, coursework or thesis.

          • Clear review.

            Each project has its own reviewers. Barchinet makes the final decision on what is accepted.

          • Privacy, adults only.

            Every contributor is 18 or older. Member names and profiles are not shown on public pages, and profiles stay off unless a member switches theirs on.
