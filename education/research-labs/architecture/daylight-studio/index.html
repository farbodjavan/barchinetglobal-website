    What is real here

    The plan is drawn to scale and its areas are calculated from the same outline the 3D view uses. The sun positions are illustrative for four times of day; they are not calculated for a date or a latitude.

    What is still schematic

    Until the baked model is installed, the 3D view is a stand-in made of simple shapes with real-time light. It shows direction and shadow, not materials or bounced light.
