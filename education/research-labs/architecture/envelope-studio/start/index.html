Noor Studio Building Envelope Lab

Build wall, roof and floor assemblies layer by layer. Calculate R and U, compare them with cited benchmarks and export a report.

Enter the studio →

The studio is a single 3D walkthrough of about 11 MB before compression. It shows its own progress bar while it loads; on a mobile connection this can take a while.
