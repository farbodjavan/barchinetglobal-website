Stair Geometry

Explore the geometry of a straight stair flight.

Android · preparing for release

What it does

Architects and designers comparing early stair layouts.

You enter
Floor-to-floor rise and a proposed riser count and tread depth.
You get
Riser height and the horizontal run of a straight flight.

Worked example

For a 2.8 m rise and 16 equal risers, each riser is 175 mm. With 15 goings at 280 mm, the run is 4.2 m.

Know the limits

Geometry only. It does not approve compliance, headroom, landings, accessibility or structure. Check the rules for the project location.
