Metal Weight

Estimate the weight and material cost of metal stock.

Android · preparing for release

What it does

Designers, fabricators and people comparing stock quantities.

You enter
Stock shape, dimensions, length, quantity, material density and optional price per weight.
You get
Calculated weight per item, total weight and an optional cost estimate.

Worked example

A 1 m × 1 m × 10 mm solid plate at 7,850 kg/m³ has a calculated mass of 78.5 kg.

Know the limits

Ideal dimensions and assumed density. Manufacturing tolerances, coatings and fittings affect actual weight; this does not assess load capacity.
