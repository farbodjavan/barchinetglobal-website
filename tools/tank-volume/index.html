Tank Volume

Estimate liquid volume and filling or draining time.

Android · preparing for release

What it does

Architects, project teams and people planning water storage.

You enter
Internal dimensions, tank shape and liquid level; constant net flow rate for time estimates.
You get
Capacity, liquid volume, remaining space and an estimated fill or drain time.

Worked example

A rectangular tank measuring 2 m × 1 m × 1 m holds 2,000 L. At 50% it contains 1,000 L; adding 500 L at a constant 25 L/min takes 20 minutes.

Know the limits

Uses ideal tank geometry and a constant net flow rate. Wall thickness, changing pressure, pump curves and obstructions are not modelled.
