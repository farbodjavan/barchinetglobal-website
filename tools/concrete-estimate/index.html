Concrete Estimate

Estimate concrete volume, material allowance, bags and cost.

Android · preparing for release

What it does

Architects, contractors and project owners planning small concrete quantities.

You enter
Rectangular or round pad dimensions, thickness, reserve allowance, bag yield and optional unit price.
You get
Net and allowance-adjusted volume, bag quantity and a material cost estimate.

Worked example

A 4 m × 3 m slab at 0.10 m thickness needs 1.2 m³. A 10% reserve brings the estimate to 1.32 m³.

Know the limits

A quantity estimate, not structural design. Bag yield and prices must match the product and supplier; waste and site conditions vary.
