Contributor terms

These terms apply when you contribute to a Barchinet project. You accept them when you request work access to a project in the project workspace.

They are provisional. Barchinet is preparing its full contributor agreement and will replace this version.

1. Who agrees

These terms are between you and Barchinet Inc. You must be 18 or older to accept them.

2. What a contribution is

A contribution is anything you submit to a Barchinet project through the workspace: drawings, models, text, data, code, images and review comments.

3. You own your part, and Barchinet may use it

You keep ownership of each contribution you make, including your research, ideas and parts of ideas, drawings, models, text and data.

When you submit a contribution, you give Barchinet Inc. a worldwide, permanent, royalty-free and non-exclusive licence to use, copy, change, combine with other work, publish and distribute it as part of the project and of products built from it. Because the licence is non-exclusive, you can still use your own contribution elsewhere, for example in your own research.

The licence stays in place for contributions Barchinet has accepted, so the project can keep using them if you stop contributing.

4. Your earlier work

Material you made before or outside the project stays yours. If you include it in a contribution, Barchinet may use it as part of the project.

5. Credit

Barchinet names you on the project for contributions it accepts, unless you ask not to be named.

6. Portfolio and academic use

You may show and cite your own contributions in a portfolio, CV, coursework, thesis or academic paper, naming the Barchinet project. For material the project has not made public, ask the project lead first.

7. Your promises

The work you contribute is your own. You are allowed to contribute it under the rules of your university or employer. Where you use material from someone else, you identify it and its source.

8. No payment, no employment

Contributing is voluntary and unpaid under these terms. It does not make you an employee or partner of Barchinet. You can stop contributing at any time.

9. If a product reaches a market

Before a product built from the project is released commercially, for example on Google Play or the App Store, Barchinet offers each member of the contributing team a separate written agreement that sets what the team receives. These terms do not promise an amount.

10. Record of contributions

Barchinet keeps a record of accepted contributions and who made them. It is the basis for credit and for the later agreement. The website privacy policy describes how the website handles data.

11. Conduct and access

Barchinet or a project lead may end your access to a project. Contributions already accepted stay with the project, and your credit for them stays.

12. Changes to these terms

These terms are provisional. When Barchinet replaces them, you will be asked to accept the new version before you contribute again.

13. Where you can use Barchinet Campus

You may not use the interactive parts of Barchinet Campus (sign-up, tools, mentoring and project work) while you are physically located in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria or the Crimea region of Ukraine, or in any other place where US sanctions law prohibits it.

You may still list education you completed anywhere in your profile. Where you live now is chosen from a list that leaves these places out.

Questions

Ask before you accept if anything here is unclear: contact Barchinet.
