03 / Make the trade-off explicit

The extra desk
is only part of the decision.

Residents set priorities. Architects and engineers turn those priorities into options and check their implications.

OPTION A

Use the shared area at agreed times

Fewer physical changes; work and shared routines may compete.

OPTION B

Create a defined work zone

A more stable work setting; less flexible shared space and new servicing questions.

OPTION C

Explore an additional module

More separation; major resource, connection and construction questions. A research possibility only.

Residents’ priorities→Architectural options→Engineering assessment→Shared decision

Implementation would depend on professional review, appropriate technology and clearly assigned responsibilities. Resident participation is limited to permitted activities suited to their training.
